Architecture:
- Metadata-first deterministic migration remains the primary execution path.
- True multi-target Medallion graph supports one source object producing Source, Bronze, Silver and approved Gold targets.
- Fact/dimension inference uses PK/FK relationships, column shape, naming evidence and downstream consumer evidence.
- Downstream consumer analysis captures direct/transitive SQL consumers and allows explicit external consumer registration.
- Gold artifacts are generated only from APPROVED business semantic definitions.
- Stored procedures/functions are handled as governed logic assets; triggers remain architecture-review items.
- Ollama remains optional and is reserved for semantic remediation, not for inventing business semantics.
